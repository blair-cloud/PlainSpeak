Set up your local AI model

Connect a local model to start explaining documents.

  1. 1Install and open Ollama for Windows.
  2. 2In PowerShell, run:
    ollama pull gemma3:1b
  3. 3Come back here and choose “Check again”.

This downloads the model to your computer. The first download needs an internet connection.

Privacy & careful use

Your document stays in the page until you choose Explain. Then this local app sends it to Ollama running on your computer. PlainSpeak does not save it.

What should I double-check?

AI can misread dates, amounts, and instructions. Compare each result with the source and contact the organization that issued the document. PlainSpeak is not legal, financial, or official advice. Use sample or non-sensitive documents while evaluating the app.
